Parameters

Score every parameter from 1 to 100. 100 = best skin, 1 = worst (the same direction as the app).
Choose a parameter: Q W E R T Y (or Tab), or click its card.
Choose the shape for that parameter: 1 circle · 2 line · 3 free curve. Each parameter remembers its own shape. 4 or X = eraser.
Circle: click one end of the circle's width, then move: the opposite end follows your pointer. Click again to finish (or press, drag, release).
Line: click the start, click the end (or press and drag).
Free curve: press, draw, release.
Eraser: click a mark to remove it, or drag across several.
Esc undoes (and cancels a shape you are in the middle of). Ctrl+Y redoes.
Zoom: pinch on the touchpad, mouse wheel, or + -; 0 to fit.
Look around at the same zoom: two-finger scroll on the touchpad, the ← ↑ → ↓ keys (hold Shift to go faster), or hold Space and drag.
Change photo: PageUp PageDown or [ ], or the buttons (← → also work when the photo is not zoomed).
Work saves automatically. A photo turns green when all 6 scores are set.
