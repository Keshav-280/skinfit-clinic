Parameters

Score every parameter from 1 to 100. 100 = best skin, 1 = worst (the same direction as the app).
1-6 choose parameter · V select / delete mode · Del remove selected · Ctrl+Z undo
Circles (acne, scars, pigmentation, under-eye): press on the centre, drag out to the edge. A single click makes a small circle.
Lines (wrinkles, sagging): click the start, click the end (or press and drag). Esc cancels.
Zoom: mouse wheel · Move: hold Space and drag, or right-drag · ← → change photo.
Work saves automatically. An image turns green when all 6 scores are set.
